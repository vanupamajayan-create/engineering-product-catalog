1.1 INFLUENCE OF REINFORCEMENT ON SEISMIC PERFORMANCE
When anchoring under seismic categories C1 and C2, the presence of closely spaced 
reinforcement bars can restrict the formation of a full concrete breakout cone. 
If structural anchoring is executed in a region with high-density rebar mesh, 
the characteristic bond resistance must be adjusted.

1.2 MINIMUM SPACING AND EDGE DISTANCE CALCULATION VALUES
For the HST4 M12 mechanical anchor, the baseline layout values are fixed to prevent 
premature splitting failure during torque execution:
* Minimum allowable anchor-to-anchor spacing (s_min) is exactly 60 mm.
* Minimum allowable concrete edge distance (c_min) is exactly 50 mm.
* Standard effective embedment depth (h_ef) is exactly 70 mm.

If the actual edge distance is compressed below the critical edge distance threshold, 
the geometric reduction factor (f_A) must be calculated linearly. Failing to apply 
the f_A modification factor will result in an over-estimation of the structural 
concrete cone breakout capacity.
