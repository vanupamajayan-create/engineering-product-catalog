HST4 High-Performance Expansion Wedge Anchor

The Nexus HST4 represents the industry standard for torque-controlled mechanical expansion anchors, specifically optimized for cracked concrete structural environments and high-stress seismic design categories (C1 and C2 certification).

Core Product Applications

  • Seismic bracing structural attachments for heavy MEP channel runs.
  • Structural steel baseplate attachments under high dynamic cyclic loading.
  • Safety-critical structural fastenings in nuclear, infrastructure, and heavy industrial facilities.

Baseline Mechanical Parameters

The structural holding capability of the HST4 relies on precise installation parameters. For standard concrete grades (C20/25 through C50/60), the mechanical thread clearance profiles must conform strictly to European Technical Assessment guidelines.
